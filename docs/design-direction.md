# VOID creative direction

The identity uses the supplied official metallic V and full wordmark in an obsidian voxel environment. The artwork's amber edges and champagne highlights guide the palette and restrained lighting. The landscape is original SVG/CSS artwork, not game footage. No borrowed Flarial assets or code.

Tokens: warm black #080706, panel #15120f, edge #30291e, gold #e8bd62, highlight #ffe4a4, white #f7f4ea. Manrope carries headings and body copy; editorial display sizes use tight tracking, with comfortable body line lengths. The supplied logo and requested black-and-gold palette guide the shared theme.

Homepage layout: compact floating navigation; left-aligned monumental hero copy balanced by the official V and voxel terrain; a quiet product-context strip; wide interface preview; asymmetric feature panels; modules and community; closing wordmark and footer. The upgrade retains this existing composition. On mobile, copy leads and artwork moves behind/below it. No fabricated statistics or features.

Gold marks interactive affordances and the supplied branding. The account portal uses the same official artwork with a focused form; the storefront uses a gallery composition with honest unpublished inventory. Geometric heading masks, varied reveals and warm depth extend the existing design without adding fake benchmark claims or distracting letter-by-letter effects.

Motion: one coordinated hero entrance; native scrolling with restrained GSAP ScrollTrigger transforms for depth; IntersectionObserver section discovery; Framer Motion for tab indicators, reveal masks, dialogs, and changing content. Stagger only related content. Disable parallax/magnetism and remove all nonessential motion for reduced-motion users. No scroll hijacking.
